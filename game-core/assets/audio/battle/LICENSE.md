# Battle audio samples

The MP3 files in this directory are trimmed, normalized, mono derivatives of selected CC0 recordings.

- Rapid-fire, explosive, beam, and heavy-impact samples: **Impacts/Gun/sfx** by Eldritch Grim — https://opengameart.org/content/impactsgunsfx
- Rapid-fire muzzle samples: processed from **Bullet Hit Metal** by coolguy244e — https://freesound.org/s/267893/
- Light bullet-impact samples: four separated variants from **bullet ricochet.wav** by aust_paul — https://freesound.org/s/30932/
- Heavy-cannon samples: `cannon_fire.ogg` from **Battle at sea** by Thimras — https://opengameart.org/content/battle-at-sea
- Tracking-missile/rocket samples: **Rocket Launch** by Jarusca — https://freesound.org/s/521377/
